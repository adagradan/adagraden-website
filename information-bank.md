## 6. Finance and transparency (Verified, CONST)

**Sources of income (Art. 8):**
1. Freewill donations and contributions from members
2. Grants and aid from individuals, corporate bodies or government agencies
3. Fundraising events and community projects
4. Membership levies and other lawful means

**Use of funds (Art. 9):** funds may only be used to achieve the Foundation's aims and objectives.

**Accounts (Art. 10):** the Foundation keeps a bank account in its own name and three registers:
1. Income register
2. Cash receipts and payment register
3. Bank lodgement and payment register

It maintains strict documentation of its finances.

**Audit (Art. 11):** independent, qualified, licensed auditors are appointed annually. The audited report goes to the AGM, and the audited statements are filed with the CAC with the annual returns.

**Non-profit clause (Art. 13):**
- Income and property are applied solely to the Foundation's objectives.
- Nothing is distributed to members as profit.
- Governing-body members receive no salary, apart from out-of-pocket expenses, reasonable rent or reasonable fees for services.
- On dissolution, remaining assets go to a similar institution or a charitable object.
